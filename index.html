What is Kickoff Buddy?

Your first World Cup, made simple.

Kickoff Buddy is an AI-powered companion for people who are new to soccer. It does not predict scores, pick winners, or replace the referee. Instead it explains — in plain, friendly language — what is happening on the pitch, why a decision was made, why the momentum shifted, and how to enjoy the experience without feeling lost.

Built with a three-stage AI workflow: brainstormed with ChatGPT, then architected and coded by IBM Bob, then reviewed, debugged, refined, and API-connected by Claude Code. Powered by two live APIs: football-data.org for match data and OpenAI GPT-4o for all explanations.

How to use

Three steps to feel confident

1
Set your context

Tell Kickoff Buddy your knowledge level, when you are watching, and which match you have selected. This shapes every explanation the AI gives you.

2
Pick a feature

Use the navigation bar above to jump to the feature you need right now — a full match guide before the game, a quick question during the match, or a matchday checklist on the way to the stadium.

3
Read, ask, and enjoy

Every AI response is written for beginners. If something is uncertain, Kickoff Buddy says so clearly and tells you where to find the official answer.

Rule Explainer

Understanding Offside

One of the most confusing rules in soccer — explained visually.

Rule: Offside FIFA Law 11
The rule in one sentence: When the pass is played, if an attacking player is closer to the goal than the last outfield defender, they are offside.
Why VAR takes so long: VAR draws precise lines from each player's body to check if even a shoulder or knee is past the last defender. It is millimetre-level, which is why it can take several minutes.
Good analogy: Imagine a relay race — you cannot start running until the baton is passed. If you're already ahead when the baton is thrown, that's the equivalent of offside.
